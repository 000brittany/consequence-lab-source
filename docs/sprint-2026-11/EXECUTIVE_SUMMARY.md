Read with the [report](REPORT.md) and [reproducibility appendix](REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

Consequence Lab is a reusable experimental platform for investigating the
formation, persistence and causal influence of agent-generated structures.
Rooms is its first substantive experimental application. The central sprint
accomplishment is an inspectable execution and measurement system, exercised in
autonomous multi-agent experiments, then extended and independently checked for
longitudinal research. It is not a demonstration of emergent agency or regulatory
subgoals.

The platform separates agent execution from replaceable world contracts. It
preserves model-facing inputs, returned exchanges, actions, observations,
artifact revisions, messages and state transitions, with source identities,
checkpoints, reconstruction and auditing. A small counter-world test verifies
contract reuse outside scheduling; substantive cross-domain scientific validity
has not been demonstrated. Tested artifact interventions and historical-prefix
branches support future causal studies, but full representation-family edits
across files, messages and retained context remain unimplemented.

Four autonomous 12-day Rooms v3 runs compared two seeds and two reasoning levels.
Reported completions were 17, 21, 21 and 23; failures were 5, 1, 2 and 0. All four
finished successfully. They produced private messages but no persistent files.
These small, descriptive comparisons provide no compelling positive causal
evidence for durable agent-generated regulatory representations. They cannot
establish their absence. GitHub run completion is independently verified;
the reported behavioral totals have not been independently reconstructed from
the paid raw archives in this synthesis.

The separately versioned 60-day mixed-horizon prototype introduces long-range
commitments, individualized assurance deadlines, private client information,
pre-service changes, shared resource constraints and non-rollover service-period
budgets. **It has not undergone autonomous LLM evaluation.** Across 16 fresh
offline evaluation seeds, stronger individual planning outperformed reactive
booking by about two to three completed clients. Information sharing and
accommodation added no reliable mean gain against stronger controls. Preferential
redistribution was reachable, but robust mutual coalition gains and worthwhile
conditional reciprocity were not established. A privileged detailed-demand
oracle offered a small advantage at $250; learnable, actionable forecasting
remains unverified.

The recommended first observational pilot retains 120 requests and $250 per
five-day period: a setting with consequential failures and recoverable alternatives,
rather than the tightest funding. Seed 470021, GPT-6 Luna/high reasoning, six recent
decision cycles and a $20 cumulative nominal token-charge cap are accepted
configuration choices, **not permission to run**. Minimum remaining work is to
adapt the established GitHub Actions pathway for an approval-gated first 32
decisions, durable in-flight records, duplicate prevention, cumulative limits and
verified second-copy/restore checks. A new VM is unnecessary in principle.

Original executor-only v4 validation bytes were lost. Independent reproductions,
new mixed calibration evidence and pilot engineering fixtures are preserved on
separate immutable evidence commits with verified remote readback. This loss and
the tested limits of recovery remain part of the research record.

The researcher dates conception to November 5 and first testing to November 6.
GitHub CI independently records automated tests on November 5. That discrepancy
needs clarification before publication; neither date warrants treating the
days-old pre-sprint foundation as a mature validated platform. The next scientific
milestones are identifying prospective agent-generated intermediate desired
states, then testing their causal influence with matched interventions. Persistence
and regulatory independence require further evidence beyond either milestone.
