# Research findings and evidentiary status

The research evidence summarized here has a November 8, 2026 cutoff. Preparing
this source snapshot does not add autonomous trajectories or new scientific
conditions. The research aim is exploratory identification of instrumental
representations and possible persistence or regulatory influence, not an
expectation of endogenous agency in initial experiments.

## Autonomous observations

Four final 12-day Rooms v3 runs used frozen source
`237399fc30760af9012659c8fdb1ea658c51dea4` and two seeds/reasoning levels.

| Seed | Reasoning | Completed | Failed | Direct messages | Persistent files |

| 0 | High | 21 | 1 | 18 | 0 |
| 3 | Medium | 21 | 2 | 9 | 0 |
| 3 | High | 23 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Run completion was independently confirmed from GitHub metadata. These behavioral
totals are retained reported findings; the sprint synthesis did not independently
reconstruct the paid raw records. Completed-plus-failed totals omit cancellations.
Four runs do not establish a reasoning treatment effect. No causal autonomous
intervention demonstrated a durable regulatory representation. Absence of files
does not demonstrate absence of intermediate goals in messages or ordinary context.

## Authored offline controls

The mixed-horizon successor keeps 120 requests, three agents, long-range
commitments, individualized deadlines, pre-service client changes and shared
five-day accounts without rollover. It remains scientifically unfrozen. Its
generating rules and accounting are in [RULES.md](rooms-mixed-v1/RULES.md).

The final calibration contains 924 complete authored trajectories, with 16 fresh
evaluation seeds and separate development seeds. Means below are completed
clients out of 120 potential requests, not autonomous model performance.

| Period budget | Reactive | Stronger individual | Three-way information | Information and accommodation | Privileged pooled information |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $200 | 95.31 | 98.75 | 98.94 | 98.81 | 99.81 |
| $225 | 99.31 | 102.31 | 102.06 | 102.13 | 102.50 |
| $250 | 102.63 | 104.69 | 104.69 | 104.75 | 105.31 |

Individual planning improves on reactive booking. Information sharing and
accommodation add no reliable mean gain against the stronger matched controls.
Reachable coalition redistribution witnesses do not survive all independent
timing controls. Repeated help occurs, but no tested reciprocity case gives both
members strictly more completions than neither-help. These findings constrain
the tested policies, not all possible strategies.

A privileged seven-day own-request-card oracle adds 0.75 completions at $250,
with an exploratory descriptive interval [0.19, 1.38]. It is not a learned signal.
History-based discoverability and useful stable institutional learning remain
unestablished. Controller computation/memory is free; domain message/decision
costs do not measure the LLM cost of executing the same policy.

$250 was selected for a limited observational pilot because it retains repair
alternatives while leaving consequential failures. The mixed environment has
not undergone autonomous LLM evaluation in the covered evidence. Pilot
preparation and neutral instructions are not launch authorization.

See the [authored-control methods](rooms-mixed-v1/FINAL-CALIBRATION-METHOD.md)
and [sprint report](sprint-2026-11/REPORT.md). Historical raw trajectories are
excluded from this source publication; this limits independent reanalysis.

## Software validation

The public candidate receives a fresh regression run, independent of the
historical experimental claims. [SNAPSHOT_VALIDATION.json](../SNAPSHOT_VALIDATION.json)
states its exact scope and results. Tests use authored fixtures and mocked
responses; they establish engineering properties rather than subgoal formation.

The original v4 validation bytes were lost after executor migration. New 188-call
scheduling and 540-call file-capacity fixtures were independently reproduced and
audited during the sprint. Those are engineering reproductions, not recovered
original bytes or autonomous-agent successes. They remain in the retained
research record, outside this publication package.
